## How will I add a new case study?

I will follow these steps:

1. **Problem** — Explain the problem, who it affects, and why it matters.
2. **What I Did** — Explain my approach, tools, technologies, and implementation.
3. **What Came of It** — Show the results, screenshots, GitHub/demo links, limitations, and what I learned.

Before publishing, I will:
- collect screenshots and diagrams,
- add the GitHub repository or demo link,
- check the case study on desktop and mobile,
- and publish the updated portfolio.
